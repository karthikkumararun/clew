App icon

A golden ball of thread with its end left trailing: the clew that leads you out of the labyrinth. One mark, three artworks tuned to where it is seen.

MACOS MASTER · 1024 CANVAS

824 px rounded-square body inside a 1024 px canvas (100 px margin), soft shadow and a 1.5 px top highlight baked in because macOS does not add them. Used for the .icns sizes from 48 px up (including 64 px and the 2x sizes), Dock, Finder, About.

WINDOWS MASTER · 1024 CANVAS

928 px body (48 px margin), no shadow: Windows draws icons edge to edge on the taskbar and Start. Used for the .ico sizes from 48 px up, the Start tile and the installer.

SMALL · BELOW 48 PX

Redrawn, not scaled down: two heavy strands instead of three fine ones, near full-bleed body, and the thread detached underneath so it never reads as a letter Q. Used for every size below 48 px (16, 20, 24, 32, 40) on both platforms.
